## Brand & Style
This design system defines a telehealth and virtual clinical environment engineered to convey authority, medical safety, and compassionate care. Telemedicine requires an immediate psychological bridge: users frequently arrive in states of vulnerability, illness, or administrative stress. The UI counters anxiety through calm structural predictability, luminous atmospheric teal gradients, soft tactile rounding, and pristine contrast compliant with strict medical accessibility standards (WCAG 2.1 AAA for vital telemetry and core workflows).

The visual language bridges **Modern Healthcare Minimalism** with delicate **Glassmorphic & Tonal Layering**. It departs from the sterile, cold blues of legacy health portals by introducing deep aquatic teals and gentle ice-blue surfaces that feel clean, contemporary, and digitally responsive. Every screen conveys an organized digital hospital counter: tranquil, lucid, hyper-legible, and respectful of both physician efficiency and patient reassurance.

## Colors
The palette balances clinical precision with reassuring warmth:

- **Primary Deep Teal (`#027368`)**: Anchors critical interactive elements, key callouts, primary buttons, active navigation states, and high-priority alerts. It provides the core visual weight and commands clinical credibility.
- **Secondary Vibrant Teal (`#03A696`)**: Applied to sub-headers, interactive badges, progress trackers, and secondary visual cues. It acts as the bridge between technical rigor and human warmth.
- **Accent Aqua (`#17BFBF`)**: Reserved for live video indicators, active telemetry pulses, and directional visual focal points. Used sparingly to preserve visual hierarchy.
- **Surface Tint / Soft Ice Blue (`#BDEDF2`)**: Functions as a soft tonal canvas for medical appointment cards, patient triage alerts, prescription panels, and container highlights.
- **Neutral Charcoal Slate (`#0F172A`)**: The primary text color. It replaces harsh absolute blacks to maximize readability while preventing ocular fatigue during prolonged screen exposure.
- **Backgrounds (`#F8FAFB`, `#F2F2F2`, `#FFFFFF`)**: Layered neutral surfaces establishing distinct container depth without relying on aggressive shadow boundaries.

## Typography
Typography is architected around typographic rhythm and instant diagnostic scanning.

- **Headlines (Montserrat)**: Provides an authoritative, geometric modernism that commands respect on clinical titles, portal onboarding screens, and key patient status cards. Bold and semi-bold weights define clear visual landmarks.
- **Subtitles and Body (Open Sans)**: Exceptional aperture clarity and neutral humanist proportions ensure reading comfort for long electronic health records (EHR), prescription dosages, and chat transcripts across any screen density.
- **Labels, Badges & Microcopy**: Form tags, appointment status badges, and vitals readouts combine Montserrat for structured button labels with Open Sans for crisp data indicators. Numeric information (dates, blood pressure, lab values) defaults to tabular figures (`tnum`) for flawless column alignment.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout relies on a 12-column adaptive fluid grid for desktop and tablet clinical views, transitioning to an ergonomic single-column view on mobile devices.

- **Breakpoints**: Mobile (`< 640px`), Tablet (`640px – 1024px`), Desktop (`> 1024px`), Ultra-Wide Clinical Workstation (`> 1440px`).
- **Consultation Interface**: Designed with fixed-width contextual toolbars (360px for EHR charts / telemedicine video controls) and fluid diagnostic central canvases.
- **Spacing Rhythm**: Governed by an 8pt architectural scale (`0.25rem`, `0.5rem`, `1rem`, `1.5rem`, `2.5rem`). Micro-spacers (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) bind label-to-input pairings and metric labels to their respective numerical values. Section paddings (`space-xl`) grant ample breathing room, reducing visual fatigue for medical staff operating multi-hour shifts.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth in this system avoids heavy, muddy drop shadows in favor of **Tonal Layering** accompanied by **Subtle Tinted Ambient Radiance**:

1. **Base Floor (Canvas Level 0)**: Background `#F8FAFB` establishing a clean foundation.
2. **Surface Tier 1 (Cards, Modules, Patient Rows)**: Solid pure white `#FFFFFF` outlined with an ultra-fine border `1px solid #E2E8F0` and an ambient diffuse shadow `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(2, 115, 104, 0.05)`.
3. **Surface Tier 2 (Flyouts, Dropdowns, Date-Time Pickers)**: White elevated containers with `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)` and subtle primary border accenting.
4. **Surface Tier 3 (Modals, Emergency Call HUDs, Video Overlays)**: Translucent glassmorphism using `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`, bordered by `rgba(189, 237, 242, 0.6)`.
5. **Interactive Glow**: Active and focused health states utilize a gentle teal outline glow `0 0 0 3px rgba(3, 166, 150, 0.25)` to guarantee immediate keyboard and tactile navigation clarity.

## Shapes
A balanced roundedness (`roundedness: 2` / base `0.5rem` to `1.5rem` for large panels) conveys accessibility, softness, and modern clinical approachability.

- **Inputs, Buttons, and Data Cells**: Styled with `border-radius: 0.5rem (8px)` to maintain structure while eliminating aggressive corners.
- **Clinical Cards, Diagnostic Blocks & Patient Banners**: Styled with `border-radius: 1rem (16px)` to create welcoming, contained modules.
- **Pill Elements**: Tags, health status chips (e.g., "Consulta Confirmada", "Aguardando Triagem"), and filter pills utilize full pill shapes (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to immediately distinguish classification metadata from interactive operational controls.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Background `#027368`, text `#FFFFFF`, font Montserrat SemiBold (14px). Hover transitions to `#03A696` with a slight elevation increase. Active state `#014d45`.
- **Secondary**: Outlined `1.5px solid #03A696`, background transparent, text `#027368`. Hover state fills with `rgba(189, 237, 242, 0.25)`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost**: Text `#027368`, transparent background. On hover, displays an ice-blue wash `#BDEDF2` at 40% opacity.
- **Emergency / End Call**: Background `#DC2626`, text `#FFFFFF`, high-contrast iconography.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Standard inputs feature a `44px` minimum touch target, `1px solid #CBD5E1` border, white background, and `#0F172A` text.
- Focus state triggers an immediate crisp border `#03A696` along with the teal focus ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(3, 166, 150, 0.2)`).
- Validation states display explicit inline icons: deep emerald (`#059669`) for verified fields (e.g., valid CPF/CRM or medical record ID) and crimson (`#DC2626`) with explicit assistive error text for required missing fields.

### Status Chips & Badges
- Small, pill-shaped tags (`9999px` radius) with `12px` font weight 600.
- **Scheduled / Agendado**: Tinted surface `#BDEDF2` background with `#027368` text.
- **Active Consultation / Ao Vivo**: Soft rose `#FFE4E6` background with pulsating `#E11D48` telemetry dot.
- **Completed / Finalizado**: Soft emerald `#D1FAE5` with `#065F46` text.

### Clinical Cards & Records
- Structured with pure white background, `16px` border radius, subtle divider lines (`#F1F5F9`), and nested metadata grids.
- Hover states on interactive patient cards elevate the container by `-2px` with a highlighted teal border tint (`#03A696`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size `20x20px` with a `4px` corner radius for checkboxes, full circle for radios.
- Unchecked: `1.5px solid #94A3B8`.
- Checked: `#027368` fill with crisp white checkmark/dot and a subtle ice-blue outer stroke.

### Specialized Telemedicine Components
- **Video Call Controls HUD**: Dark frosted glass surface (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), containing circular 48px toggle controls for mic, camera, screen-share, and instant prescription generation.
- **Medical Vitals Tracker**: Modular ice-blue cards (`#BDEDF2` background at 30% opacity) grouping clinical units (BPM, SpO2, Blood Pressure) with bold Montserrat figures and high-legibility Open Sans unit markers.
